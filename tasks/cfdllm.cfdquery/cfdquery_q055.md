If \( \vec{f} \) is the body force of an infinitesimally small element (volume \( dx\, dy\, dz \) and density \( \rho \)) moving along with the flow (velocity \( \vec{V} \)), which term is the work done by the body force?

1. \( \vec{f} \cdot \vec{V} \; dx\, dy\, dz \)
2. \( \rho \vec{f} \cdot \vec{V} \)
3. \( \rho \vec{f} \cdot \vec{V} \; dx\, dy\, dz \)
4. \( \rho f \; dx\, dy\, dz \)

Answer with only the number (1, 2, 3, or 4).
