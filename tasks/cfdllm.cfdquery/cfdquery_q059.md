The relationship between the rate of heat transfer per unit area \( \dot{q}_s = -k \nabla T \). Where, \( k \) is a scalar value of thermal conductivity and \( \nabla T \) is the gradient of temperature. Which of these following is wrong according to the above equation?

1. Heat transfer is different in different directions
2. The rate of heat transfer depends upon the temperature gradient
3. Heat transfer is in the opposite direction of increasing temperature
4. k is the proportionality constant

Answer with only the number (1, 2, 3, or 4).
