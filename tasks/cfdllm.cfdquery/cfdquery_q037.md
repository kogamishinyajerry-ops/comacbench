1. \( \nabla \cdot (\rho \vec{V}) = 0 \)
2. \( \nabla \cdot (\vec{V}) = 0 \)
3. \( \text{div}(\vec{V}) = 0 \)
4. \( \text{curl}(\vec{V}) = 0 \)

Answer with only the number (1, 2, 3, or 4).
