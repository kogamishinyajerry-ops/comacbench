Consider an infinitesimally small fluid element with density \( \rho \) (of dimensions dx, dy, and dz) fixed in space and fluid is moving across this element with a velocity \( \vec{V} = u \hat{i} + v \hat{j} + w \hat{k} \). The net mass flow across the fluid element is given by \_\_\_\_\_

1. \[ \left( \frac{\partial (\rho u)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (\rho v)}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial (\rho w)}{\partial z} \right) dx \; dy \; dz \]
2. \[ \frac{\partial (\rho u)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (\rho v)}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial (\rho w)}{\partial z} \]
3. \[ [\rho] dx \; dy \; dz \]
4. \[ \left( \frac{\partial (\rho)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (\rho)}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial (\rho)}{\partial z} \right) dx \; dy \; dz \]

Answer with only the number (1, 2, 3, or 4).
