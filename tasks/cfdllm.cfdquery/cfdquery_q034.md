Consider an infinitesimally small fixed control volume with density \( \rho \) (of dimensions dx, dy, and dz) fixed in space and fluid is moving across this element with a velocity \( \vec{V} = u \hat{i} + v \hat{j} + w \hat{k} \). The rate of change in mass of the fluid element is given by \_\_\_\_\_

1. \( \frac{\partial (\rho u)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (\rho v)}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial (\rho w)}{\partial z} \)
2. \( \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} \)
3. \( \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} (dx \; dy \; dz) \)
4. \[ \left( \frac{\partial (\rho u)}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial (\rho v)}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial (\rho w)}{\partial z} \right) dx \; dy \; dz \]

Answer with only the number (1, 2, 3, or 4).
