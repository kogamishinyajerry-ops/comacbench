The general transport equation is \( \frac{\partial (\rho \Phi)}{\partial t} + \text{div}(\rho \Phi \vec{u}) + \text{div}(\Gamma \nabla \Phi) + S \). For Eulerian equations, which of the variables in the equation becomes zero?

1. \( \Gamma \)
2. \( \rho \)
3. \( \Phi \)
4. \( \vec{u} \)

Answer with only the number (1, 2, 3, or 4).
