1. \( \tau_{yz} = \mu \left( \frac{\partial v}{\partial z} + \frac{\partial w}{\partial y} \right) \)

3. \( \tau_{yz} = \mu \left( \frac{\partial v}{\partial z} + \frac{\partial w}{\partial x} \right) \)
4. \( \tau_{yz} = \mu \left( \frac{\partial w}{\partial z} + \frac{\partial v}{\partial y} \right) \)

Answer with only the number (1, 2, 3, or 4).
